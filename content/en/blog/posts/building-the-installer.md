Installing an operating system is the first impression. We wanted ours to take four screens and fewer than ten minutes.

:::steps

### Language and keyboard

Detected automatically, confirmed with one tap.

### Installation type

Erase disk, install alongside, or advanced partitioning.

### Encryption

On by default, with a printable recovery key.

### Account

Create your user and you are done.

:::
