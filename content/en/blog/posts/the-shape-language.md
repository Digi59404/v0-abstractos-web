The AbstractOS mark is a slash, a circle and a triangle. They are the simplest shapes we could find that still feel like they have a personality.

## The palette

| Token | Role | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brand-blue` | Primary | Actions, focus, links |
| `brand-coral` | Accent | Highlights and illustrations |
| `brand-sun` | Accent | Warnings and warmth |
| `brand-teal` | Accent | Success and tips |

## Motion

Shapes move with a single easing curve, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`, for 200 to 300 ms. Circles scale, slashes slide, triangles rotate. That is the whole system.
