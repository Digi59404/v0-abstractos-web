Turbine is the default web browser. It blocks known trackers and third‑party cookies out of the box, with no setup required.

![The Turbine browser showing a new tab page](/screenshots/turbine.webp){width=1920 height=1200}

## Protection levels

| Level | Trackers | Third-party cookies | Fingerprinting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Blocked | Partitioned | Reduced |
| Strict | Blocked | Blocked | Blocked |
| Off | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed |

:::tip
If a site breaks under **Strict**, use the shield icon in the address bar to relax protection for that site only.
:::
